Important: this is a browser-based prototype. Data is not automatically shared with your supervisor or other users. For a multi-user production system, add secure login, a central database, role permissions and scheduled cloud backups.

Recommended controls

  • Unique invoice number checking to reduce duplicate purchase entries.
  • Approval before issue for higher-value or restricted items.
  • Expiry / batch tracking for items with shelf life.
  • Department-wise monthly consumption and purchase analysis.
  • Audit trail recording who entered, approved and issued each transaction.
  • Physical stock verification and adjustment records.
